# Career Strategy

Updated: 2026-08-26

## Objective

Move from Tuff Shed into a materially larger role that properly values the scope already being performed: enterprise product/platform leadership at the intersection of CPQ, manufacturing technology, configuration-to-production, enterprise applications, and operational transformation.

This is not a search for another conventional product-manager role.

The strongest fit is an organization with meaningful operational complexity where configuration, data, workflow, integrations, and physical production must work as one system.

## Target Role Families

Primary:

- Head of Product / Platform
- Senior Director, Product
- Senior Director / Director, Enterprise Applications
- Head of Platform & Production Systems
- Director / Head of Manufacturing Technology
- Principal / Director, Business Transformation
- Product/platform leadership in design-to-build or configuration-to-production ecosystems

Secondary:

- Strategic consulting leadership
- Principal product roles with enterprise-wide scope
- Transformation roles centered on manufacturing or complex commerce

## Positioning

Core narrative:

> I build systems that turn complexity into clarity and make that clarity operational.

Differentiators:

- Understands CPQ as an enterprise operating system rather than merely quoting software.
- Connects commercial configuration decisions through engineering and production outcomes.
- Builds visibility where organizations are otherwise operating blind.
- Comfortable taking control of fragmented vendor and application ecosystems.
- Operates effectively across product, engineering, manufacturing, finance, operations, and executive leadership.
- Can stabilize a troubled environment while simultaneously defining its future-state architecture and governance.
- Strong bias toward making the whole system visible before optimizing individual components.

- 165+ locations

- large-scale manufacturing operations
- multiple configuration engines and transition-state architectures
- CPQ, visualization, workflow, ERP, production and reconciliation systems

The next role should recognize this as enterprise platform leadership.
