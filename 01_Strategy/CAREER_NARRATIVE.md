# Career Narrative

## One-Line Positioning

Enterprise product and platform leader specializing in CPQ, configuration-to-production, manufacturing systems, and operational visibility.

## Short Version

I tend to get pulled into environments where the technology stack has grown faster than the organization's ability to see or govern it. My work is about making that system visible, stabilizing it, and then creating the product, data, workflow, and governance structure that lets the business scale.

At Tuff Shed, that meant working across CPQ, configuration, visualization, workflow, production and ERP systems supporting a national manufacturing and retail footprint. The role evolved into something much broader than traditional product management: effectively connecting configuration decisions to real production outcomes while coordinating internal teams and a substantial vendor ecosystem.

## What Makes the Story Different

The differentiator is not familiarity with one CPQ product.

It is the ability to reason across the entire configuration-to-production chain:

customer need
→ product configuration
→ pricing / quoting
→ visualization
→ workflow
→ engineering intent
→ BOM / production output
→ manufacturing
→ reconciliation / operational visibility.

## Leadership Pattern

Repeated pattern:

- enter ambiguity
- map the system
- expose hidden dependencies
- establish a shared operational picture
- challenge dangerous shortcuts
- align vendors and internal teams
- stabilize delivery
- create governance
- scale

## Interview Theme

A useful recurring phrase:

> Before you can govern a complex system, everyone has to be able to see the same system.

This connects naturally to the Broncos interviews, the Tuff Shed experience, and future enterprise-platform roles.
