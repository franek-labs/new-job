# Retirement & Benefits Transition

## 401(k)

Questions to resolve:

- plan administrator / provider
- employment separation processing date
- current balance
- vesting status if applicable
- outstanding loan status if applicable
- rollover options
- whether any immediate action is actually required

General principle: do not rush a rollover until plan details and next destination are understood.

## ESOP

Tuff Shed is employee-owned.

Obtain:

- latest vested balance / statement
- distribution rules after separation
- valuation timing
- payment timing
- tax treatment documentation
- contact information for plan administrator

## Health Benefits

Confirm:

- exact final date of active coverage
- COBRA election timing
- COBRA premium
- alternative coverage options and enrollment windows
